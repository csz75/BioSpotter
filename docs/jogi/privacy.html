5. Location Data

The location of an observation, together with the other details of that observation, is sensitive data. Two different things follow from this.

Who can see your observations

  • If you do not have a Premium subscription, you can only see your own observations. Your observations are not shown to anyone else.
  • If you do have a Premium subscription, you can see the observations of other users as well. This means that the observations you record — including the species, the photograph and the location — are visible to Premium subscribers, whether or not you are a Premium subscriber yourself.

Making observations visible to other users is a fundamental part of what BioSpotter is: it is what the service is for, and it is the reason the observations are stored at all. The legal basis for this processing is therefore the performance of the contract between you and us — not a separate consent.

This means you can opt out of it at any time by deleting the individual observation or your entire account (Section 8). It also means that a basic observation, once recorded, may be visible to Premium subscribers before you delete it, so please only record observations that you are comfortable sharing in this way.

What we never do with location data

  • we do not use location data for advertising, profiling or tracking
  • we do not publish precise coordinates of sensitive or protected species in a way that would make them easier to find or disturb

8. Deleting Your Account

You can delete your account at any time from within the application (Settings → Delete Account).

When you delete your account:

  • your name and email address are deleted and replaced with a randomly generated identifier
  • you can register again later using the same email address
  • your observations are retained in anonymized form — the species, location and date remain, but can no longer be linked to you personally
If you want your observations to be permanently deleted as well, manually delete them from your observations list before deleting your account.

We retain billing data after account deletion due to legal obligations.
