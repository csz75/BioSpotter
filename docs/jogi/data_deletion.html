This page describes what BioSpotter does when you delete your account.

How to delete your account

  1. Open Settings
  2. Scroll to the bottom and choose Delete account
  3. Confirm the deletion

What happens after deletion

DataWhat happens
Email addressdeleted and replaced with a random identifier
Display name, profile picture, biodeleted
Sign-in to the accountceases (password invalidated)
Your former email addressfreed up – you can register again with the same address
Your observationsremain in anonymous form (species, location, date, photo)
Your subscriptionswitches to the free plan and does not renew
Billing dataremains due to legal obligations (8 years)

If you want to delete everything

If you want your observations to disappear as well, do this BEFORE deleting your account:

  1. Open the My observations list
  2. Open each observation
  3. Delete them one by one

Then delete your account – that way nothing is left behind.

Why the anonymous data remains

BioSpotter is a scientific database. Observations – species, location, date – are valuable even when the person can no longer be linked to them. This is why anonymisation is an accepted form of deletion: the personal data ceases to exist, the scientific data remains.
